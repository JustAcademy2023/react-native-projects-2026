Ten levels, one rule: finish each one.

These react native app ideas for beginners start tiny and grow into real products. Every build teaches one clear lesson, so nothing feels overwhelming.

1

Counter app

A plus button, a minus button and a number. It teaches state, the heart of every react native app.

Lesson: useState
2

Tip calculator

Inputs, percentages and a result line. Your first taste of validating what people type.

Lesson: inputs and layout
3

To do list

Add, finish and delete tasks. Meet FlatList and the habit of never changing an array directly.

Lesson: lists and keys
4

Notes app

A list screen and a detail screen. Learn how data stays in sync when you go back.

Lesson: navigation
5

Weather app

Call a public API, show a loader and plan for a dead network. Delhi weather keeps it fun.

Lesson: async code
6

Recipe finder

Search, images and a detail page, with network code tucked into a small service file.

Lesson: clean data flow
7

Expense tracker

Save amounts on the device and add up monthly totals. Real data shape, real summaries.

Lesson: storage and context
8

Habit tracker

Streaks, dates and daily reminders. Time zones will test your patience in a good way.

Lesson: dates and permissions
9

Shopping app with cart

Products, quantities, totals and a fake checkout. The closest rehearsal for a real job.

Lesson: app wide state
10

Chat or feed app

Add a Node backend with sign in, posts and messages. Your bridge to full stack work.

Lesson: auth and backends
